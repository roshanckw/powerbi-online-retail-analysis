# Power BI-online-retail-analysis

# Project Overview

Developed an end to end Power BI analytics dashboard for a UK e-commerce retail dataset. The project covered product performance analytics, customer segmentation, churn tracking, and sales forecasting across UK and international markets.Tools used : Power BI , DAX.

# Dataset

- UCI Online retail II dataset
- Scope: ~500K transactions, Dec 2019- Dec 2020, UK + internatinal customers

# Key analysis

- Top five best-selling and least selling products in the UK
- Top five best-selling and least selling products internationally
- Peak sales periods in the UK and globally

## Trends between
- unit price and number of items sold
- Invoice data and customer location
- Product description and invoice date

## Advanced analytical insights

- Customer segmanatation based on purchasing patterns (eg- high-volumne buyers, repeart customers, occasional buyers)
- Customer retantion tracking over time to identify loyal customers and potentila churn.
- Sales trend forecasting for the top five products to determine demand fluctuation.
- Seasonal sales patterns to help with inventory management and marketing campaigns.

  

# Tools Used
-Power BI
- DAX
- Excel
